# 1 · Sacral Unconscious

> *The deep base of the column. The seat of desire. What the project* wants*, before it
> knows how.*

The sacrum is the broad, fused foundation that bears the weight of everything above. Here we
articulate **desire-oriented specifications**: documents that say what is wanted and *why*,
in the language of intention rather than implementation. This is the unconscious of the
project — the wishes that drive it, surfaced and named but not yet rationalized into
architecture.

## What lives here

- Desire-oriented spec documents ("we want X so that Y")
- Problem statements and the felt need behind them
- Success conditions framed as satisfied desire, not technical acceptance criteria
- The "why" that everything upward must remain answerable to

## How to work here

- Speak in wants and reasons, not mechanisms. *Avoid* prescribing how.
- Each spec should trace back to a seed in
  [`../coccygeal_differentiation/`](../coccygeal_differentiation/) where useful.
- When a desire is clear enough to be given bones, promote it to
  [`../lumbar_architecture/`](../lumbar_architecture/).

Architecture above must always be able to point back down here and say: *this is the desire
I serve.*
