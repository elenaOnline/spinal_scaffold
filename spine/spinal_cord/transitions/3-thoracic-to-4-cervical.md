# thoracic → cervical : completed work surfaces for oversight

<!--
Special case: the destination IS the crown. The reflex part is the routine surfacing of
finished work; arrival itself hands the work to judgment, which may then emit efferent
send-backs (see efferent-send-backs.md).
-->

## What moves
A completed wave in a `thoracic_infolding/` plan surfaces to `cervical_awareness/` to be
documented and brought under oversight.

## Reflex conditions
- [ ] Wave `status: complete`.
- [ ] Its items are built and verified.

## On promotion
- Surface the wave to the crown for documentation (a cervical artifact, `kind: documentation`).
- Set the wave's built lumbar items `status: built`.
- Once a whole plan's waves are complete, set thoracic `status: complete`.

## Escalation
Arrival at the crown is itself the mediation point. The crown, now seeing the result against
the whole column, judges its health — and where it finds a fault, emits an **efferent
send-back** down the cord (see `efferent-send-backs.md`). A satisfied desire is set sacral
`status: satisfied`.
