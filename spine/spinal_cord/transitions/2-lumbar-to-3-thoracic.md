# lumbar → thoracic : the bones fold into a wave

## What moves
A sound write-up in `lumbar_architecture/` is folded into a wave in a `thoracic_infolding/`
plan as an executable item.

## Reflex conditions
- [ ] Lumbar `status: sound` (a `draft` is not eligible).
- [ ] *How it's built* is concrete enough to act on.
- [ ] No **load-bearing** entry remains in *Open questions*.

## On promotion
- Place the item into a wave in the relevant thoracic plan, linked back to its lumbar write-up.
- Set lumbar `status: building`.
- The cord handles **single-item placement** reflexively.

## Escalation
Relay to the crown (and thoracic coordination) when folding the item **changes the wave
structure of the whole plan** — altering dependencies, resequencing waves, or changing what
can run in parallel. Placing one bone is a reflex; re-choreographing the breath of the entire
plan requires sight of the whole.
