# sacral → lumbar : the want gets bones

## What moves
A desire-spec in `sacral_unconscious/` is given structure as one or more technical write-ups
in `lumbar_architecture/`. The desire remains the thing the bones must stay answerable to.

## Reflex conditions
- [ ] Sacral `status: open`.
- [ ] *The want* and *Why* are both present.
- [ ] Satisfaction is articulable (the want has a recognizable met-condition).
- [ ] The *Tensions* field, if present, is empty or resolved.

## On promotion
- Create a lumbar write-up from the bones template, with `serves:` linked to this desire.
- Set sacral `status: architected`.
- One desire may spawn several lumbar write-ups; each links back to the same desire.

## Escalation
Relay to the crown when giving the want bones requires a **structural decision that crosses
other features or the architecture as a whole** (a shared contract, a cross-cutting concern),
or when *Tensions* names an unresolved conflict with another want. Local frontmatter cannot
tell whether a structural choice coheres with the rest of the column — that is the crown's.
