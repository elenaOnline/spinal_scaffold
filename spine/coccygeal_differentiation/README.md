# 0 · Coccygeal Differentiation

> *The vestigial tail. The seed. The closest point to the earth and the deep past.*

This is where work **begins as undifferentiated matter** — raw ideas, half-formed updates,
speculative futures. The coccyx is a remnant of phylogeny: it carries the trace of where
the organism came from. Here, ideation is not yet committed to desire, structure, or plan.
It is *differentiation* — the first splitting of an undifferentiated possibility into a
nameable thing.

## What lives here

- Loose ideation notes and brainstorms
- Proposed future updates and directions
- Speculative "what if" fragments not yet owned by a spec
- The backlog of seeds awaiting promotion
- New desire split out of in-flight feedback — the dropped end of the triage's **new-seed lane**

## How to work here

- Write freely. Nothing here is a commitment.
- One idea per file is fine; so is a running notebook.
- When a seed clarifies into a *want*, promote it upward into
  [`../sacral_unconscious/`](../sacral_unconscious/) as a desire-oriented spec.

Seeds ascend **out** of this folder — and new ones are also *born* here: the feedback triage's
**new-seed lane** drops genuinely new desire back to the base rather than fast-tracking it into a
live wave. Once promoted, a seed rarely descends back in.
