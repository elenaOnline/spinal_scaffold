# Spinal Development

A development paradigm — not a project, but the **foundation for the development of projects**.

Spinal Development uses the human spine as a model for project architecture. Drawing
on *Spinal Catastrophism* (Thomas Moynihan / after Reza Negarestani's milieu), it reads
the upright body as a tension between two poles:

- **Down** — toward the earth. The **coccyx and sacrum** root the project in the grounded
  past, in phylogeny: where things come from, the raw deposit of desire and origin.
- **Up** — toward the sky. The **cervical vertebrae** reach for a zenith, the *telos* of
  ontogeny: awareness, oversight, the realized form a project grows into.

Development is the ascent along this column. Work begins as undifferentiated matter at the
base and climbs, vertebra by vertebra, into conscious, governed execution.

## The Ascent

```
        ▲  cervical_awareness      sky · telos · ontogeny · oversight
        │  thoracic_infolding      breath · the wave of implementation
        │  lumbar_architecture     load-bearing structure
        │  sacral_unconscious      desire · the unconscious want of the project
        │  coccygeal_differentiation   earth · origin · the vestigial seed
```

Matter enters at the coccyx as raw, undifferentiated idea. It is given voice as desire in
the sacrum, given bones in the lumbar, given breath and motion in the thoracic, and finally
given eyes in the cervical crown. Nothing skips a vertebra; the column bears load only when
each level is intact beneath the next.

## The Cord

Threaded through every vertebra — but belonging to none, and so unnumbered — is the
[`spinal_cord/`](./spinal_cord/). The vertebrae are where artifacts *settle*; the cord is the
*movement between them*. It carries **three streams**: **afferent-promotion** (upward, internal),
**efferent send-backs** (downward, internal), and **afferent-sensory** (inward, external) — the
**dorsal horn** ([`spinal_cord/afferent-sensory/`](./spinal_cord/afferent-sensory/)), where
feedback from the world enters at the active segment and is sorted by a four-lane triage into one
of the existing paths. (Afferent-sensory is a third *origin*, not a third direction — direction
stays pure.) Each handoff runs in one of two modes: **reflex** (local, evaluable from frontmatter
alone, no crown involvement) or **mediated** (relayed up to the cervical crown for whole-system
judgment, with the ruling returned down the cord). The crown *integrates and judges*; the cord
*transports and reflexes* — complementary organs, never one doing the other's work. The crown's
standing rules for changing the cord — the coherence invariants and the movable, always-insertable
human-gate — live in
[`cervical_awareness/cord-stewardship.md`](./cervical_awareness/cord-stewardship.md).
