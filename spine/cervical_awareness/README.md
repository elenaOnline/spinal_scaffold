# 4 · Cervical Awareness

> *The crown of the column. The neck that holds the head toward the sky. The zenith — the
> telos of ontogeny.*

The cervical vertebrae lift the skull toward the sky; they are the seat of orientation and
of looking. This is the layer of **awareness**: guidelines, procedures, documentation, and
oversight. Where the base is grounded in the past, the crown faces the project's realized
form and watches over the whole column beneath it.

## What lives here

- Guidelines and conventions for the whole paradigm
- Procedures and runbooks
- Documentation of what has been built and why
- Oversight: review standards, quality gates, retrospectives

## The standing documents

Beyond per-project records, this layer holds the paradigm's standing guidelines — the parts a
fresh project inherits unchanged:

- [`template-guide.md`](./template-guide.md) — the shape of every artifact, vertebra by vertebra,
  plus the cord's own `transition` and `feedback` artifacts.
- [`cord-stewardship.md`](./cord-stewardship.md) — when and how the cord's transitions may change,
  the coherence invariants, and the **movable oversight line** (autonomy by default; a `gate: human`
  insertable on any item, wave, or plan).
- [`execution-topology.md`](./execution-topology.md) — which work runs inline, which spawns a
  judgment subagent, which fans out into parallel builders, and where a raised human-gate routes to
  a person.

## How to work here

- This layer **sees the entire spine**. It is the only layer entitled to comment on the
  health of the levels below it.
- Documentation here should remain answerable all the way down to the desire in
  [`../sacral_unconscious/`](../sacral_unconscious/): does the realized form still serve
  the original want?
- Oversight findings can send work back down the column — a flawed wave to
  [`../thoracic_infolding/`](../thoracic_infolding/), a missing bone to
  [`../lumbar_architecture/`](../lumbar_architecture/), or an unmet desire back to the
  sacral base.
- Oversight runs **autonomy-by-default**: the crown lets reflexes, wave amendments, and its own
  send-backs proceed untouched, and is consulted only at the edges (verifying finished work,
  admitting new desire) or wherever a human-gate is raised. Corrective *feedback* is the crown
  speaking — it recognizes a drift and emits the send-back; the cord never returns work on its own.

This is the zenith. From here, the project looks back at itself and knows what it is.
